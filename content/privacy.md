_Last updated: 2 October 2026_

This website is operated by Vangera Systems. It is designed to collect as little personal data as possible.

## What is collected

**Contact form.** When you send a message, your name, email address and message are emailed to Vangera Systems together with your IP address and browser type (used to prevent abuse). Messages are not stored in a database and are used only to reply to your enquiry.

**Spam protection.** The form uses [ALTCHA](https://altcha.org), a privacy-friendly check that runs in your browser. It sets no cookies and sends nothing to third parties.

**Server logs.** The web server records technical access logs (IP address, date and time, page requested, browser type) for security and troubleshooting. They are rotated automatically.

## Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or third-party trackers.

## Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing [info@vangera.systems](mailto:info@vangera.systems).
