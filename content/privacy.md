_Last updated: 12 October 2026_

This website is operated by Vangera Systems. It is designed to collect as little personal data as possible.

## What is collected

**Contact form.** When you send a message, your name, email address and message are emailed to Vangera Systems together with your IP address and browser type (used to prevent abuse). Messages are not stored in a database and are used only to reply to your enquiry.

**Spam protection.** The form uses [ALTCHA](https://altcha.org), a privacy-friendly check that runs in your browser. It sets no cookies and sends nothing to third parties.

**Server logs.** The web server records technical access logs (IP address, date and time, page requested, browser type) for security and troubleshooting. They are rotated automatically.

## Visit statistics

To see which pages are read and how people find this website, it can count visits with **Google Analytics**, but **only if you allow it**. A bar at the bottom of the page asks first. Until you choose **Allow**, nothing from Google Analytics loads and no cookies are set. Your choice is remembered in your browser (not in a cookie), and you can change it at any time with **Visit statistics** at the bottom of every page. Choosing **No thanks** later turns the statistics off and removes their cookies.

If you allow it:

- Google Analytics records the pages you view, how you arrived (for example from a search or a link), your approximate location (country and city, worked out from your IP address), your device type, browser, screen size and language, and how long you stay.
- It sets two cookies, `_ga` and `_ga_<id>`, which last up to two years unless you remove them.
- Google says Google Analytics 4 doesn't store IP addresses. Google Signals and the advertising features are turned off, so this data isn't used for ads.
- Google processes the data on its servers, which may be outside Laos. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [how Google uses data from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- We see only totals and trends in Google Analytics, not who you are.

## Cookies and tracking

Apart from the visit statistics above, and only if you allow them, this website sets no cookies and uses no advertising or other third-party trackers.

## Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing [info@vangera.systems](mailto:info@vangera.systems).
